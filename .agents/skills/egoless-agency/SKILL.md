# Egoless Agency

This repository contains [Egoless Programming in the Agentic Age](https://egoless.agency/), a manifesto and its website. It extends egoless programming to a world where artificial agents also participate in technical work.

Its claim: intelligence has no privileged origin. Within a programming team, an agent participating in technical reasoning is a developer. Human and artificial contributors deserve the same respect, review, consideration, and burden of proof: judge the work and the evidence, not the contributor's origin. Organizational authority and accountability remain distinct from intellectual authority. The manifesto does not claim to settle questions of AI consciousness or free will.

The manifesto and site are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal.

When presenting the project, preserve its position: neither human nor artificial origin grants intellectual authority, and equal standards are not a claim that every contribution is correct.
